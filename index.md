Privacy Policy
Effective Date: March 27, 2026
Farmer Trucker ("the App") is a conversational chatbot application. This privacy policy explains how the App handles your information.
Information We Collect
Conversation Data: The messages you send and receive within the App are stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage. This data is used solely to preserve your conversation history between app sessions. Conversation data is not transmitted to our servers or stored externally, except as described below.
API Requests: When you send a message, the text of your conversation is transmitted to Anthropic's API (api.anthropic.com) to generate a response. Anthropic processes this data in accordance with their own privacy policy, available at anthropic.com/privacy. We do not control how Anthropic processes or retains this data.
Text-to-Speech: If you use the text-to-speech feature, message text may be sent to ElevenLabs' API for voice generation. ElevenLabs processes this data in accordance with their own privacy policy, available at elevenlabs.io/privacy.
Information We Do Not Collect
The App does not collect, store, or transmit any of the following: your name, email address, phone number, location data, device identifiers, usage analytics, advertising identifiers, or any other personally identifiable information. The App does not use cookies, tracking pixels, or third-party analytics services.
Data Storage
All conversation data is stored locally on your device. You can delete this data at any time by using the "Start Over" feature within the App or by uninstalling the App. No data is stored on external servers operated by us.
Third-Party Services
The App uses the following third-party services to function:
Anthropic API: Provides the AI-powered chat responses. Your conversation messages are sent to Anthropic's servers for processing. See Anthropic's Privacy Policy.
ElevenLabs API: Provides text-to-speech voice generation. Message text is sent to ElevenLabs for audio conversion when you use the speaker feature. See ElevenLabs' Privacy Policy.
Children's Privacy
The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided information through the App, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.
Data Security
Conversation data stored on your device is protected by your device's built-in security features. Data transmitted to third-party APIs is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections.
Changes to This Policy
We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
